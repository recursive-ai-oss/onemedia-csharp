# OneMedia

**Containers, metadata, subtitles; no decoding.** A managed .NET library for media containers with no dependency beyond the .NET base class library. Unaffiliated with other products or companies named OneMedia.

**Preview (`0.8.0-preview.4`): reads MP4/MOV/3GP (including fragmented MP4), Matroska/WebM, AVI/OpenDML and DV, ASF/WMV/WMA, MPEG-TS/M2TS, PS/VOB and elementary streams, MP3/ADTS/AC-3/AMR files, Ogg, FLAC, FLV, WAV/BWF/RF64/W64, AIFF/AIFC and CAF; writes MP4/MOV/3GP/fragmented MP4/CMAF, Matroska/WebM, MPEG-TS/M2TS and WAV/BWF/RF64; remuxes, trims, splits and concatenates without re-encoding; edits tags losslessly; makes files playable in browsers; strips location and device identifiers (the fields listed in its documentation; it does not search free text, unknown vendor atoms or trailers); writes capture dates; and repairs damaged files and rebuilds cut-off MP4/MOV/3GP recordings into a new file with a report; and packages HLS and DASH (CMAF or transport-stream segments, playlists and an MPD, video on demand or live).** Packets with exact timestamps, keyframe flags and source byte ranges; codec header parsing (H.264, HEVC, AV1, VP8/VP9, AAC, Opus, Vorbis, FLAC, AC-3 and more); native tags (ID3, APE, Vorbis comments, RIFF INFO, ASF and more) and a common tag view; zero-allocation packet pull and structural-only probing of huge or remote files.

**Previews, subject to change (subtitle parse and convert speed is published; the other previews have no speed rows yet):** subtitles and captions (SRT, WebVTT, ASS, TTML, SBV, LRC, container text tracks, CEA-608/708, SCC, teletext, bitmap subtitles), capture facts as evidence, telemetry and timed events. Facts and date writes were checked against ExifTool and MediaInfo on published samples and generated files, and on 193 freely licensed device recordings (capture time 189 of 189, location 18 of 18 against ExifTool and MediaInfo; https://github.com/recursive-ai-oss/onemedia-csharp/blob/main/docs/device-corpus.md). The CTA-608/708 and SCTE 35 standards were not available, so those parts rest on public regulations and black-box oracles. Repair is checked on generated files, published samples, 10 GoPro clips and 12 device models cut and rebuilt; with a reference the recovered bytes equal the original's, without one only files with parameter sets in the stream recover. **HLS/DASH packaging is a preview and has not passed a validator** (Apple `mediastreamvalidator` was not run; the DASH-IF conformance tool ran once and then fails in its own base-URI handling): it is verified by packet equality with ffprobe and by playback in hls.js and dash.js in Chromium, Firefox and WebKit plus native HLS in macOS WebKit. **HLS/DASH presentations and growing files as sources are previews:** live DASH is not reloaded, HLS subtitle renditions are not read, a segment is held whole in memory up to `MediaLimits.MaxSegmentBytes`, and keyed segments are reported `Protected` and not decrypted.
